# Time and results

How long it takes to reach the band you want depends on two things: your general
English level, and the time you put into practice and tasks. Those same two
things answer whether there is time before your exam date, and how far it is
from the band you have to the band you want. It is discussed in the
consultation.

How much time you need each day also depends on your level and on how long you
have until exam day.

No band score is promised here. But if you work on the material and the practice
you are given and follow the plan, every effort is made to get you to the score
you want.
