# Cancelling, refunds and answering hours

## Cancelling or moving a session

You can cancel or move a session up to 24 hours before it starts.

## Refunds

If you cancel more than 24 hours before the session starts, you are refunded.

## If you miss a session

The course carries on from the next session. If you want the missed one made up,
a new time is arranged with you.

## Answering hours

Shabnam answers between 9am and 3pm.

A reply usually takes under half an hour.

## Prices

Prices are not published on the site yet. Ask Shabnam directly for a price.
