# Courses

### Skills for Band Score 6.5

This course is for someone who needs IELTS band 6.5, and your general English
level must be at least B1.

This course covers all four skills — Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking —
and is taught online, one to one. The number of sessions a week is arranged with you, and
two a week usually works well. How long the course runs depends on how often you
meet and how fast you progress.

### Skills for Band Score 7 and Above

This course is for someone who needs IELTS band 7 or higher, and your general
English level must be at least C1.

This course also covers all four skills — Reading, Listening, Writing and
Speaking — and is taught online, one to one. The number of sessions a week is arranged
with you, and two a week usually works well. How long the course runs depends on
how often you meet and how fast you progress.

### How the two differ

The difference is where you start: the 6.5 course begins at B1, the 7-and-above
course begins at C1.

Which one fits you depends on two things — your general English level now, and
the band you need. The placement assessment establishes both, and the
consultation is where it is discussed.

### Grammar and Vocabulary

The Grammar and Vocabulary course is pre-recorded and open to anyone, and there
is no requirement to join it. It covers all the grammar and vocabulary you need
for the IELTS exam.

### Plan Tracker

With Plan Tracker you are given a plan built around your English level and the
time you have. That plan comes with a tracker, so you can follow your own
progress.

### Business English

Business English is the language used in different professions rather than
preparation for IELTS, and it is pre-recorded.

### AI & IELTS

The AI & IELTS course has not been decided yet, and the details will be

### If you do not want IELTS

The IELTS courses prepare you for that exam, and general English is not taught
in them.

If the exam is not your goal and you simply want your English to improve, the
Grammar and Vocabulary course can help. Business English also exists, but that
is the language of the workplace rather than general English.
