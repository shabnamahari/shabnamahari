# Courses

## Skills for Band Score 6.5

For someone who needs IELTS band 6.5.

**Requirement:** your general English level must be at least B1.

**Covers:** all four skills — Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking.

**Format:** online, one to one.

**Sessions:** arranged with you. Two sessions a week usually works best.

**Length:** depends on how often you meet and how fast you progress.

## Skills for Band Score 7 and Above

For someone who needs IELTS band 7 or higher.

**Requirement:** your general English level must be at least C1.

**Covers:** all four skills — Reading, Listening, Writing and Speaking.

**Format:** online, one to one.

**Sessions:** arranged with you. Two sessions a week usually works best.

**Length:** depends on how often you meet and how fast you progress.

## How the two differ

The difference is where you start: the 6.5 course begins at B1, the 7-and-above
course begins at C1.

Which one fits you depends on two things — your general English level now, and
the band you need. The placement assessment establishes both.

## Grammar and Vocabulary

This course is offline and open to anyone. There is no requirement to join it.

It covers all the grammar and vocabulary you need for the IELTS exam.

## Plan Tracker

You are given a plan built around your English level and the time you have.

It comes with a tracker, so you can follow your own progress.

## Business English

This is about the English used in different professions. It is not IELTS
preparation.

It is offered offline.
