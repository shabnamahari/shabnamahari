# Inside a session

Sessions are held online, on Google Meet, and each one runs for an hour and
fifteen minutes.

Sessions are not recorded, so you cannot watch one again afterwards.

You are given a task for each session. The Writing tasks can be sent to Shabnam
outside class hours and she corrects them. You are also sent a few good Writing
samples to work from.

Your session time is fixed and does not change from week to week.

Being outside Iran is not a problem, as long as the class can be held during the
day, Iran time.

You do not need to buy a book or any materials. What you need is provided, and
it will be on the site soon. If anything else is needed, you are told in class.
