# Payment, cancelling and refunds

One-to-one sessions are paid ten at a time, in one payment, before the sessions
start.

The pre-recorded courses are purchased, so they are paid for in full.

You can cancel or move a session up to 24 hours before it starts. If you cancel
more than 24 hours before, you are refunded.

If you miss a session, the course carries on from the next one. If you want the
missed session made up, a new time is arranged with you.

If after a few sessions you feel the course is not right for you, that is
something to take up with Shabnam directly.

Prices are not published on the site yet. Ask Shabnam directly for a price.


## Answering hours


Shabnam answers between 9am and 3pm, and a reply usually takes under half an
hour.
